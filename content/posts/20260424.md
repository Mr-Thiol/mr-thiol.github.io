## Work Submitted!

This study focuses on utilizing Large Language Models to predict the scalability of Metal-Organic Framework (MOF) syntheses. In this project, I was primarily responsible for designing and creating the figures to visually demonstrate our core concepts! You can view the preprint by clicking the title here: <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20899" target="_blank">Predicting Scale-Up of Metal-Organic Framework Syntheses with Large Language Models</a>.

I am beyond honored to have collaborated with the <a href="https://zhenglab.wustl.edu" target="_blank">Zheng Group</a> to bring this project to fruition.

Words cannot fully express my gratitude to Zach! He offered me this incredible opportunity and taught me so much about crafting elegant, scientifically rigorous data visualizations. I have learned a lot from our daily discussions on Slack!

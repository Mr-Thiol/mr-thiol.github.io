<h4 style="text-align: center;">Hongrui "Sam" Sheng</h4>

Hi! I am a senior majoring in Chemistry at **Fudan University**. My research interests lie at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Chemistry, with a strong focus on utilizing data-driven approaches to accelerate materials discovery.

My research began with AI-assisted electrocatalyst design (advised by Prof. Ming Gong), and I later moved on to using active learning to optimize the crystallinity of MOFs (advised by Prof. Zhiling (Zach) Zheng). Along the way, I have grown increasingly interested in the *de novo* design of reticular materials, especially with generative models.

**Recent whims:**
1. Can Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures (JEPA; LeCun, 2022) be brought into chemistry?
2. Can we develop a unified representation for both 2D and 3D COFs, as a stepping stone toward future generative models?
